WG-3.75 scope

WG-2, WG-3, and WG-3.5 run once on the coarse topology. Their accepted categorical and continuous physical fields are then inherited to the requested finer topology through stable WG-1 ancestry and deterministic geodesic provenance.

Fine boundary interfaces retain the accepted tectonic kind, geological regime, subduction polarity, convergence/divergence rate, shear rate, and source-boundary provenance needed by WG-4 terrain.

The Earth-like physical profile remains the default while water inventory, ocean density, isostatic mantle density, heat flux, and mantle thermal expansivity are explicit inputs for future rocky-planet profiles.

No elevation, bathymetry, sea-level solve, climate, hydrology, erosion, resources, Regions, Features, or gameplay cutover are generated in WG-3.75.
