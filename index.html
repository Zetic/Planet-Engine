Physical diagnostics

WG-4 scope

The solid surface combines crustal isostatic support, oceanic age subsidence, collision/ridge/rift/subduction morphology, inherited basin tendency, broad mantle support, and lithospheric mechanical filtering.

Earth-like surface-water mass is converted to a physical volume and solved against the generated basin geometry to derive sea level, bathymetry, and initial land/ocean distribution.

This is pre-erosional tectonic topography. No climate, drainage, river incision, sediment transport, glaciation, detailed lithology, resource deposits, Regions, Features, or gameplay cutover are generated here.
